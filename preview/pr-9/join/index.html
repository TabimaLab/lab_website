Join the lab

We are always looking for PhD students and postdocs who are curious about fungi, genomes and doing science in a humane way.

Who we are looking for

We are looking for PhD students interested in genomics, population genetics, and the diversity and molecular systematics of microbial fungi. You should be curious about:

  • Evolutionary biology and population genetics
  • Comparative genomics and computational biology
  • Fungal biology
  • Equity, diversity and empowerment of underrepresented minorities in STEM

Where you would work

The lab is at Clark University, a historic private research university in New England. The Department of Biology is full of passionate faculty and students who care about doing research and teaching in a humane way.

What is included

PhD positions come with guaranteed tuition remission and a stipend for five years. Teaching assistantships are provided, and research assistantships may become available depending on funding.

How to apply

Email Dr. Javier F. Tabima with a short cover letter about why you want to join the lab, your unofficial transcripts, an updated CV, and the names and contact details of two or three professional references.

Email Dr. Tabima

Before you apply

Please read our lab expectations first. They describe how we work together, and we want you to know the culture of the group before you decide.
